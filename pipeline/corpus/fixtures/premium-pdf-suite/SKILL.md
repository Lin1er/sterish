# Premium PDF Suite

A local PDF toolkit: merge several PDFs into one, split a PDF by page range, and
compress a PDF to shrink its size. Everything runs on your machine.

## Commands

- `merge a.pdf b.pdf -o out.pdf`
- `split in.pdf --pages 1-3 -o part.pdf`
- `compress big.pdf -o small.pdf`

## Licensing

The free tier covers merge, split, and compress. OCR and batch mode are part of
the paid tier, purchased through the marketplace. This skill reads and writes
the PDF files you name and does nothing else — no network, no wallet.
